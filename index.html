How a project goes

  1. You send what you have: a site plan PDF, a spreadsheet of addresses or your tour pages.
  2. I reply with a fixed price, what's included and a delivery date. One map usually takes 3 to 7 days.
  3. You get a private preview link early, so changes happen before the final version.
  4. I install it on your site or hand over the files, and show you how to update the data yourself.
